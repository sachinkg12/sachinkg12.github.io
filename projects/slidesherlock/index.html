Concept diagram · Slide-to-video pipeline

The problem

A fluent narration can introduce facts that were never present in the source slides. Reading bullets verbatim avoids some risks but often makes a poor explanation.

How it works

Extract slide content into an evidence index, generate narration linked to that content, apply heuristic checks, and use a template fallback when needed. Assemble the narration and rendered slides into video.

What is available

Released as a Python package and Docker-based application. The GroundLM 2026 workshop paper evaluates narration over 30 decks and 279 slides.

Scope and limitations

The checks are proxies for selected errors. They do not establish that every narrated statement is correct or eliminate hallucination.
