Speaker biography

Sachin Gupta is a backend engineer and independent researcher in San Jose, California. He is a Member of Technical Staff 2 at eBay, working on billing infrastructure, APIs, and AI enablement. His independent work includes HeapLens, TRACE, RouteGuard, AgentTrustCI, and ToolContractGate, with papers in ACM, IEEE, and EMNLP workshop proceedings.
