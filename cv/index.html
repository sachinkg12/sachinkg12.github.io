Sachin Gupta is a backend engineer and independent researcher in San Jose, California. He is a Member of Technical Staff 2 at eBay, working on billing infrastructure, APIs, and AI enablement. His independent work includes HeapLens, TRACE, RouteGuard, AgentTrustCI, and ToolContractGate, with papers in ACM, IEEE, and EMNLP workshop proceedings. He delivers keynotes and technical talks about reliable AI agents, evidence-grounded language systems, and developer tools. Views are his own.
